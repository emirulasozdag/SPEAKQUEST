Privacy

SpeakQuest is a course project. This page says in plain words what the game keeps about you, where your messages go and how to delete everything.

Do not type real personal information

The characters in the city are played by an AI service. Use made-up names, places and details in your conversations, never your real address, phone number, ID or anything private.

What we store

  • Your account: your e-mail address and your password, which is kept only as a secure hash (nobody can read it back).
  • Your character: name, avatar, English level and learning goal.
  • Your conversations: the messages you write and the characters' replies, quest by quest.
  • Your learning data: the language mistakes found in your messages with their corrections, and your skill scores.
  • Your progress: quests, XP and level, your story decisions and how much each character trusts you.

Your data is only shown to you. Other players can never see it.

Where your messages go

Every message you send in a conversation is passed to an AI service (Google Gemini) together with the scene, so the character can answer and your English can be checked. Your e-mail address and account details are never sent.

The game uses the free tier of this service. On the free tier, Google may use the messages to improve its products. That is why you should only write things you would not mind a stranger reading.

Speaking instead of typing

On the conversation screen you can press Speak and say your answer. Your browser turns your voice into text in the message box; nothing is sent until you check the text and press Send.

SpeakQuest never receives, records or stores your voice. Some browsers (for example Chrome) use their maker's online speech service to turn speech into text, under that browser's own privacy terms. Reading replies aloud happens on your device and is off until you turn it on.

How to delete your data

  • Start again: Profile → Reset game progress deletes your quests, conversations, mistakes, skill scores, story decisions and trust. Your account and character stay.
  • Delete everything: Profile → Delete account asks for your password and then permanently deletes your account and all of the data above.
