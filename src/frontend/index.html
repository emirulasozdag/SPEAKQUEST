How a quest works

  1. Talk to people

    Characters in the city answer what you actually write, not a multiple-choice script.

  2. See your mistakes

    Grammar is checked separately from the conversation, so an understandable answer still moves the story.

  3. The story adapts

    If you keep slipping on the past tense, your next quest gives you a reason to practise it.
